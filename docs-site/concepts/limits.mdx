---
title: "Hard limits"
description: "What the contract enforces whatever the model says."
---

The wallet contract enforces these on every `execute`, after the verdict. The model can only make a payment stricter than they allow, never looser.

| Limit | What it does | Set by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Per payment** | the most one payment may send, per token, and in NEAR | owner: `set_token`, `set_near_caps` |
| **Per day** | the most all payments may send in one UTC day, per token, and in NEAR | owner, same calls |
| **Receiver allowlist** | which contracts the agent may call, such as `wrap.testnet` for wNEAR | owner: `allow_receiver`, `disallow_receiver` |
| **Allowlisted signers** | which TEE signing addresses the wallet accepts verdicts from | owner: `register_signer`, `revoke_signer` |
| **Freeze** | stops every payment until the owner unfreezes | owner: `freeze`, `unfreeze` |
| **Cross-chain switch and recipients** | payments through 1Click are off by default; when on, an optional list of Base addresses | owner: `set_cross_chain`, `allow_cross_chain_recipient` |

A token payment needs caps set for that token. Without them, the wallet refuses with "no caps set for this token".

## On every proposal

- **Expiry:** an action expires at most an hour after it's proposed. An expired one can't execute.
- **Nonce:** each nonce executes at most once.
- **At most 32 pending proposals.** Expired ones are cleared with `prune`.
- **Size:** `args_json` at most 2,048 bytes, a method name at most 64 characters, and never a call to the wallet itself.

## What a wrong ALLOW can cost

If the model is fooled, the most one bad payment can send is the per-payment cap, and the most in a day is the daily cap, to a contract on the allowlist. Set the caps to what you'd accept losing in a day.

## Changing them

The owner changes the limits by signing the setters with their own NEAR account; the web app's wallet page shows the current ones under **Hard limits**. See [Manage the wallet](/guides/manage-wallet) and the [contract reference](/reference/contract).
